## hero_background.png (homepage hero)

The original hero photo of the Bahamas flag against open sky, restored after a
short-lived video experiment. Note: the file is AVIF data with a `.png`
extension; modern browsers decode it fine, but re-export as a real PNG/JPG/WebP
if anything downstream relies on the extension.
